PART I — THE ARCHITECTURE: GEOMETRY, LAYERS, FLOW, AND EQUATIONS

THE LOOP, AT A GLANCE
=====================

Layer 0 is the singularity — the CRP (Cosmic Return Point), zero, the empty
mirror, a point. But 0 is not a bare point: even as a singularity the CRP
has an asymptotic shell. The CRP is the empty space inside the shell, and
the shell is the circular spherical circumference of that empty space. 0 is
the empty space and its shell combined — one thing that is also a duality.

The empty mirror also reads socially: a scapegoat is the empty center a
group triangulates around but refuses to look into. The full reading is
in Part II — Theology.
Layer 12 is the asymptotic shell, the outer boundary. 0 and 12 are equal
opposite mirrored reflections of each other: 0 is the shell fully compressed,
12 is the shell fully expanded. They exist in unison, and the two together
are how the universe is held together. Everything between is a place the
flow passes through, out and back.

The flow, in four steps:

STEP 1 — OUT (the exhale) — the Northern Hemisphere: conscious creation
  Flow moves out from the CRP through the layers toward the shell, shaped by
  each layer it passes through.

STEP 2 — THE TURN (containment)
  At the shell the radial velocity is zero — the flow cannot cross. It turns
  around inside Layer 11 and flows around the boundary.

STEP 3 — RETURN (the inhale) — the Southern Hemisphere: unconscious generation
  The flow comes back through the lattice — the web woven by the hourglass
  and the toroidal field working together — to the CRP.

STEP 4 — REFLECT (the mirror)
  The CRP reflects the flow back out and the breath begins again. Closed loop:
  no dead end, no escape.

  Once a Product is removed from the flow, the material that supported
  it changes hemisphere in effect: what was actively being created in
  the north becomes archived material in the south, where it can either
  be built from again or deconstructed back to raw. Nothing in the sphere
  has only one life.

A note on the diagram: a cross-section of the structure is an hourglass shape
with the CRP at its center pinch. A dashed vertical axis runs from the CRP to
the shell at the top. Arrows show flow moving out from the center to the shell,
turning inside Layer 11, and returning along a curved path through the lattice
to the center. Each line across the hourglass stands for a whole spherical
shell (a 3-sphere) at that radius. The dashed vertical line is the radial
axis — the coordinate rho. It is one dimension of the structure, not the
structure.

THE EQUATIONS
=============

Ten statements carry the whole structure: where a layer sits, what shape it
is, how the flow moves on it, why it cannot leave, how it comes back, how the
layers mirror each other, where the 144 comes from, and what makes it move.

1. LAYER COORDINATE
   rho = n,  rho in [0, 12]
   rho IS the layer number: Layer n sits at radius rho = n. Layer 0 is
   rho = 0 (the CRP, the empty mirror), Layer 6 is rho = 6 (the Filter),
   and Layer 12 is rho = 12 (the shell). One number says where you are in
   the structure. But rho is not only a coordinate. Rho IS the observer's
   perspective: the all exists everywhere at once, all places, all the
   time, but an observer can only participate from its coordinate. The
   observer IS the coordinate of perspective — that is where you exist
   inside the all-structure. Avatars are not the all; they are the
   perspective of the all. All is one, one is all — but it is the universe
   and its fractal perspective. The exact form of the observer term is
   left open. [Interpretive]

2. THE LAYER-SHELL
   S^3_rho = { (z1, z2) in C^2 : |z1|^2 + |z2|^2 = rho^2 }
   Every layer is a shell — a 3-sphere of radius rho. A layer is not a flat
   ring or a line segment; it is a whole spherical world at that distance
   from the center. At rho = 0 the shell is compressed to the point (0, 0) — the
   point is the shell compressed together with the empty space;
   the shell does not disappear at 0.

3. FLOW ON EACH SHELL — THE HOPF MAP
   h(z1, z2) = ( |z1|^2 - |z2|^2 , 2 z1 z2-bar ),  linking number = 1
   On every shell at positive radius the flow circulates as a Hopf fibration:
   the shell is filled with circles, and every circle is linked through every
   other circle exactly once. Linking number 1 means the link cannot be undone
   by any smooth deformation — the weave of the lattice is unbreakable. At rho =
   0 the shell is a single point and carries no fibers.

4. CONTAINMENT — THE BOUNDARY
   v(12) = 0
   At the shell, radial velocity is zero. The flow does not slow down and
   leak out, and it is not crushed back — it simply cannot cross. That is
   why it turns. This one condition is the containment of all 13 layers.

5. THE LOOP-BACK — RETURN MAP
   R : S^3_12 -> CRP
   The return is a map, not a jump: a definite path from the outermost shell
   back to the center — the CRP, Layer 0 — travelled through the lattice.
   The shell is not the end of the flow. The flow returns.

6. THE MIRROR
   M(n) = 12 - n,  M(6) = 6
   Every layer mirrors another: 0 <-> 12, 1 <-> 11, 2 <-> 10, 3 <-> 9,
   4 <-> 8, 5 <-> 7. Layer 6 maps to itself — it is the mirror point, the
   exact middle of the structure.

7. THE FILTER
   F(intent) -> Product | Compost
   At Layer 6, the midpoint, intent splits the flow. Two states of self —
   ego and Source — breathe into one action, which becomes Product
   (Layer 7) or Compost (Layers 8-11). Intent is a human act: the equation
   gives the SHAPE of the choice, not the choice.

   ratio between Product and Compost — how much of the flow is kept, and
   how much is returned. The second dial sets how the by-products are
   distributed among the return layers. In the architecture document
   these dials are written with symbols; in this blueprint they are kept
   as what they are in the picture: two choices a human being makes,
   moment by moment, about what to keep and what to let go back into the

   Phi(rho, t + T) = Phi(rho, t)
   The flow Phi is periodic with period T — one full breath, out and back.
   After each cycle the structure is where it began, at the CRP, and begins
   again. This is the infinite looping reflection: a loop, not an expansion

9. THE 144

   Twelve faces reflecting in, times twelve reflecting out. The

   outward reflection across the whole structure.
