# SHELF-FLOW-001 — Inner stalls and candidate entrainment
2026-11-25. Exploratory model; not a physical foam claim.
A flat shelf [a,b] without drive has v=v0 exp(-gamma t), x=x0+v0(1-exp(-gamma t))/gamma. Starting at a with v0>=0, finite crossing iff v0>gamma(b-a); equality approaches b asymptotically. This applies to any exactly flat shelf, including a hypothetical [3.5,4.5] near layer 4 (not present in RETURN-MAP-001's quadratic potential). With gamma=.08, v0=.02 stalls at 3.75, .08 approaches 4.5, .12 exits in 13.732653608351375. Formula ceases to apply after exit.

For a stalled tracer in a prescribed moving carrier, add beta(U-v) to acceleration on shelf: v'=-(gamma+beta)v+beta U. For constant U, beta>0, v converges to beta U/(gamma+beta). For x0=3.75, v0=0, beta=.2, U=.15, gamma=.08, limiting velocity .10714285714285712; exits at x=4.5 at t=10.375949133426165. This is an externally maintained carrier, not self-consistent fluid evidence.

Candidate distributed model: radial mass density n and velocity u satisfy partial_t n+partial_x(nu)=0, partial_t(nu)+partial_x(nu²+p)=-n V'-gamma n u+external drive+coupling. If using shell-volume density on S³ shells, include radial area factor A(x)=2 pi² x³ and a central boundary prescription. Pressure or diffusion can disperse pooling; convergent flux can increase density; neither pooling nor entrainment is guaranteed. A kinetic distribution f(x,v,t) may better represent overlapping inward/outward streams: partial_t f+v partial_x f+partial_v[(-V'-gamma v+beta(U-v)) f]=0, with boundary kick/reflection laws. For self-consistency, U must be derived from a carrier and coupling must exchange equal/opposite momentum; pressure/energy closure and external energy supply must be specified. A stalled packet is a candidate accumulation mechanism, not proof of a bubble, interface, foam, or physical time-side interpretation.
