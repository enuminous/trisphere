# MAG-004 — Resonant Coherence: exploratory protocol
Recorded before execution, November 26, 2026 Pacific. Not independently preregistered.
Use MAG-003's periodic XY chain with H=sum(cos d1+r cos d2), target r=.5 and exact ground energy -.75/site. Reconstruct native breathing in effective time. Fix duration at 12 native effective periods and T=1.2(1-s)^2+.015. Compare static and native, sine (aligned to native fundamental), Fourier-phase-scrambled (higher harmonics randomized, fundamental retained), and block-shuffled (fixed random permutation of 16 equal phase blocks) waveforms at .5,1,2,4 times native frequency. Match discrete mean r=.5 and RMS modulation to native breathing, and match noise, initial conditions, annealing time, quench and temperature. Report range and fraction below .25: RMS matching does not match these. Surrogates each use one fixed template, not a distribution. No feedback or microscopic derivation; overdamped toy model, not spin-wave resonance.
N=24,48,96; two independent cohorts of 12 seeds per size, paired across arms. 3072 coarse steps; half-step repeat first six seeds per cohort/size with Brownian increments coupled by exact aggregation. Final L-BFGS quench at r=.5. Ground hit gap/site <1e-6. Check lower bound and optimizer status; record energy gap, wall proxy, Fourier peak, chirality, near-target bonds, moment, and angles. Check reflection with negated noise and initial angles for one seed per size/cohort and arm.
Primary: native frequency 1 vs static and the three frequency-1 controls, mean paired reduction in final gap/site, pooled equally across sizes/cohorts; one-sided paired sign-flip Monte Carlo test (50000 draws, +1 correction), Holm across four tests. Support native advantage only if all adjusted p<.05, and all effects positive in both independent cohorts and in half-step subset. Secondary hit counts, domain walls; sweep exploratory, no post-hoc best-arm significance. Preserve nulls. No EuSi predictions or validation of TriSphere. A frequency peak needs a new sealed confirmation test.
