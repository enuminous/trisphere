# TRISPHERE-MAG-003 — Formation of coherence
Exploratory protocol, written before running this implementation. No independently preregistered thresholds or material fit.

Use MAG-002's periodic classical planar spin chain, H=sum(cos d1+r cos d2), target r=0.5. Reconstruct its radial orbit with the specified potential, damping and kick energy. Compare 4 arms with common initial angles and Gaussian increments: static r=.5; common positive breathing mobility (exact matched effective-time control); differential breathing r=.5+.6*(rho/12-effective-clock-weighted mean rho/12); and a conventional staged ratio ramp r=.2+.3*s, where s is fraction of annealing time. All arms have identical mobility-clock increments and temperature schedule (T=1.2*(1-s)^2+.015). The static arm and scalar arm are the same numerical trajectory by construction: no independent validation of time change. The differential waveform is an ad hoc hypothesis, not a Blueprint-derived law. The ramp is an illustrative conventional control, not an optimized baseline. All arms return to r=.5 for the final deterministic L-BFGS quench. Use N=24,48,96, 32 seeds per size, 12 cycles; compare a paired subset of 8 seeds per size with half step (couple noise via aggregation). Also reflect the first 4 seeds and Gaussian increments in each size/arm. Report ground hits (gap/site <1e-6), peak Fourier weight, absolute mean chirality, domain wall fraction (bond sign flips), near-120 bond fraction and net moment at checkpoints and after quench. Ground energy -0.75/site exactly. Reflection is a symmetry check, not an independent sample of preference. If differential exceeds static, report paired discordance and refinement; superiority to the conventional ramp and robust convergence are not assumed. No EuSi predictions, finite-temperature transition, microscopic derivation or empirical validation. A negative or inconclusive result remains publishable.
