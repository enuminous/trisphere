# MAG-002 exploratory protocol (written before execution)
The original scalar oscillator has no map to electronic spin. Test an explicitly new extension: planar unit vectors on a periodic chain, nearest and next-nearest antiferromagnetic exchange J1=1,J2=r. No twist or chirality field in the energy. Exact Fourier lower bound, 3 sizes (24,48,96), 9 ratios (0,.2,.25,.3,.4,.5,.6,.8,1). Local L-BFGS relaxation from 12 random starts and their reflections at each size and r=.2,.5,.8. Record all outcomes, ground-state success if energy gap per site <1e-6, reflection discrepancy <1e-8 in energy and chirality (optimizer rounding can cause deviations). Compare ordered-state electron spectra using 241 physical momenta, 151 energies, exchange J=0,1, chirality reversal and collinear control. Exact spectral parity tolerance 1e-10, nonzero signal threshold .01 normalized spectral weight. Test common positive radial modulation of the entire spin energy by time-reparametrization using previously reported Eq10 orbit, beta=.6, compare same effective time. This is an added one-way coupling; no thermodynamics, temperature fit, or EuSi structural model. Pitch matching at ratio .5 is a chosen comparison, not a prediction. No new independent EFMW prediction is assumed.
