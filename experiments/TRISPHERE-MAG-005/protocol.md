# MAG-005 — Criticality and defect erasure: exploratory protocol
Recorded before execution; not independently preregistered. Test whether driving r=J2/J1 below the infinite-chain equilibrium pitch bifurcation r=1/4 hinders subsequent spiral ordering. This is a finite, driven overdamped stochastic chain, not a material phase-transition test. Use MAG-004's effective clock, 12 periods, temperature schedule, 3072 steps and final r=.5 quench. N=24,48,96, two fresh cohorts of 16 seeds per size; nested 8 seeds per cohort/size at half step, one reflection per cohort/size/arm. References: static, native 1x, sine 1x. Intervention: eight rectangular drives with low L=.18,.24,.26,.32 for 1/8 period and two compensating levels each for 7/16, computed to match mean .5 and native RMS. Repeat once or twice per native period; timing arms at each L share an identical histogram. Compensation changes with L, so this is a matched waveform family contrast, not isolation of threshold crossing alone. All arms share initial conditions, Brownian paths, duration, temperature and quench. Fine increments aggregate to coarse.
Primary outcome: final quenched gap/site relative to -.75; average paired differences over both timing patterns for each seed: deep (.18 minus .32), near (.24 minus .26). Positive means crossing worse. One-sided paired sign-flip test (50000 draws, +1 correction, assumes sign symmetry/exchangeability under null), Holm across both; unadjusted stratified bootstrap intervals (within size/cohort). Support hypothesis only if both adjusted p<.05 and both effects positive in both cohorts, both timing patterns and nested half-step subset. Otherwise not established. Report per-size/cohort hits (gap<1e-6), wall proxy, absolute chirality, Fourier peak, near-120 bond fraction, net moment, optimizer status and bound. Record intra-pulse dynamics during last four native periods: before low, after low, end of each waveform cycle. Wall sign is unreliable near collinear order; not a physical defect count. Reference arms and dynamics descriptive only; no post hoc significance, material prediction, or physical validation.
